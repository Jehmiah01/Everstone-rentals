## Business rules locked in
- Minimum age 18. Valid physical driver's license required. One-time identity + license check at first booking (customer uploads license photo + selfie holding it; owner approves in the dashboard; later bookings skip it).
- Booking flow: pick car and dates, enter driver info once, review and sign both agreements on one mobile page (typed name, drawn signature, card-authorization initials, e-sign consent), pay through Square, then upload license + selfie before pickup.
- Pickup address `2121 Gwynn Oak Ave, Gwynn Oak, MD 21207` is sent and shown only after the owner approves the reservation (constant lives server-side in `app/src/lib/config.server.ts`).
- Agreement numbers: `ECG-YYYY-NNNN`, auto-generated per booking.
- Rates (owner-confirmed 2026-10-21, all three cars): 1 day $85, 2 days $160, 3 days $225, 4 days $280, 5 days $325, 6 days $345, 7 days $350. Over 7 days: $350 per full week + table price for the remaining days (assumption, editable per car in the dashboard). Acura VIN `19UUA66257A018557`, tag `4GV8423`. The other two VIN/plates are blank until the owner fills them in.
- Company address in agreements: 100 West Road, Suite 300, Towson, Maryland 21204. All "Providence Rentals" references removed.
